About Us

Your Everyday Spot in Birkirkara

Olive3 is a café and bistro serving breakfast and lunch every day of the week, from proper coffee and fresh juices to poke bowls, pasta and burgers.

Everything is made to order in our open kitchen, with new dishes on the specials board all the time. Eat in, take away or order to your door.
